About

THE WOMAN.
THE MYTH.
THE LAUGHS.

Debbie is an Argentine-born actress and comedian who now calls London home. With a knack for finding the funny in the heartbreaking and the heart in the hilarious, she brings a unique cross-cultural perspective to every stage and screen.

From Buenos Aires to London's vibrant comedy scene, Debbie has honed her craft across continents — delivering performances full of warmth, wit, and disarming honesty.
