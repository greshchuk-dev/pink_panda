About

THE WOMAN.
THE MYTH.
THE LAUGHS.

Debbie is an Argentine actress and stand-up comedian based in London. She has been performing since the age of ten — from Buenos Aires theatre to London stages and international comedy nights.

Bilingual in English and Spanish, energetic and fearless, she brings heart and authentic storytelling to every role.
